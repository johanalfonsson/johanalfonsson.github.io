Johan Alfonsson is an associate senior lecturer at Halmstad University and is affiliated with the Department of Sociology and Work Science at the University of Gothenburg, where he received his PhD in sociology.

His research focuses mainly on work, class and precarious employment, including class theory, the conditions of the working class and alienation. He has written about on-call work and the growth of insecure employment in Sweden, about low-wage jobs and wage inequality in the Nordic model, and about how split shifts affect staff in elderly care.

Books
------
* *Vad hände med arbetarklassen? Om svek, makt och ojämlikhet i Sverige* [What Happened to the Working Class? On Betrayal, Power and Inequality in Sweden] (Arkiv förlag, 2025). On how the economic, political and social power of the working class has been eroded since the 1990s.
* *Det otrygga arbetslivet i Sverige: Dess framväxt och konsekvenser* [Precarious Working Life in Sweden: Its Emergence and Consequences] (Studentlitteratur, 2022).
* *Alienation och arbete: Unga behovsanställdas villkor i den flexibla kapitalismen* [Alienation and Work: The Conditions of Young On-Call Workers in Flexible Capitalism] (Arkiv förlag, 2020).
