Johan Alfonsson holds a PhD in sociology and is an associate senior lecturer at Halmstad University, Sweden. His research focuses mainly on precarious work, its causes and consequences, and more broadly on work, class and inequality in Sweden.

Dissertation
------
Alfonsson received his PhD in 2020 with the dissertation *Alienation och arbete: Unga behovsanställdas villkor i den flexibla kapitalismen* [Alienation and Work: The Conditions of Young On-Call Workers in Flexible Capitalism] (Arkiv förlag). The book examines the lives of on-call workers in flexible capitalism from the perspective of alienation.

Research projects
------
**Precarious work in elderly care: Split shifts and their impact on staff**  
*Postdoctoral project funded by Forte, Halmstad University, 2023–2026*  
The project examines how split shifts, where the working day is divided into two parts with a long break in between, affect assistant nurses and other staff in elderly care.

**Low-wage jobs in the Swedish labour market 2005–2020: What are the jobs, where are they, and what explains their development?**  
*Research project funded by Forte, 2021–2025*  
The project studied the growth of low-wage jobs in Sweden, who is at risk of ending up in them, and why they have emerged.
