## Precarious work in elderly care: Split shifts and their impact on staff

Postdoctoral project at Halmstad University, funded by Forte, 2023–2026.
{: .project__meta}

The project examines how split shifts affect assistant nurses in Swedish elderly care. A split shift divides the working day into two parts with a long break in between, and the project looks at how such fragmented working hours shape the balance between work and the rest of life.

## Alienation and work: Young on-call workers in flexible capitalism

Doctoral dissertation in sociology, University of Gothenburg, 2020.
{: .project__meta}

Over recent decades, capitalism in the service society has become increasingly flexible. Capital and goods move ever more freely, production runs just-in-time, and Sweden has seen a sharp rise in the number of people working under insecure conditions on temporary contracts as on-call workers. What are the consequences of this new, flexible working life for employees?

To understand the conditions of on-call workers today, Alfonsson turns to the theories of alienation that were so influential in industrial society. Drawing on Marx and the philosopher Rahel Jaeggi, he develops a theory of alienation for our time, grounded in capitalism's unfulfilled promise of self-determination. Faced with this contradiction inherent in capitalism, we are all fundamentally alienated, but how that alienation is expressed varies.

Moving attentively between theory and empirical material, Alfonsson shows how young on-call workers are limited in different ways in their ability to shape their own lives, their work, their free time and their social relationships. Those who have chosen this form of employment voluntarily, who have some form of financial security and whose work is more predictable experience a lower degree of concrete alienation. Those who lack these conditions are instead in a highly vulnerable position.

*Alienation och arbete: Unga behovsanställdas villkor i den flexibla kapitalismen* [Alienation and Work: The Conditions of Young On-Call Workers in Flexible Capitalism] portrays a flexibility shaped by the needs of capital rather than those of people, a development felt by far more people than those in on-call employment. The dissertation was published as a book by Arkiv förlag.

Awarded the Arbetsmiljöhögskolan Prize in memory of Ann Bergman for the best Swedish doctoral dissertation of 2020 in the field of working life and work environment.
{: .project__award}
</div>
